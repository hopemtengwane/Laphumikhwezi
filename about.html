Our inspiration

iKhwezi, the morning star.

A symbol of light, direction and the promise of a new day — the meaning at the heart of the Laphumikhwezi name.

A light to follow

With care through life’s hardest moments.

In isiXhosa, iKhwezi is the morning star. It appears before sunrise, offering a point of light at the edge of a new day. For Laphumikhwezi, this is a meaningful image of guidance and hope.

We are building this page to share the company’s own story, history and values. More about the people and community behind Laphumikhwezi will be added as those details are confirmed.
